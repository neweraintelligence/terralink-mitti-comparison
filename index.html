Part 1 · Mitti’s feature set, and our call

Mitti groups its product into Understand (capture), Act (execute), and Improve (learn). It runs on the web and on iPhone and Android, with a public API. The badge is the build rating. The green line is what TerraLink is doing with it.

Understand · capture

01 Inspections, checklists and reports

Build
  • Drag-and-drop builder: pages, sections, repeating sections
  • Template library, file upload, and AI draft from a photo, PDF, or prompt. Multi-language templates
  • Choices, text, number, checkbox, date and time, slider, photo and video, annotation, signature, GPS, instructions, barcode, table, formula, and site, asset, or person pickers
  • Logic, required fields, scoring, auto-flagging, and follow-up actions
  • Offline, with a time and place on every answer. Start from a QR code
  • Schedules, approvals, branded PDF, Word and Excel, share link
TerraLink. Full builder, same on the phone and the server. Hands-free voice inspections. 42 starter templates (fleet, warehouse, plant, and the regulatory pack), English and French on the fleet set. No public marketplace of 9,000 templates.

02 Issue and observation reporting

Build
  • Any worker raises an issue: category, photos, location, asset
  • AI turns a photo, voice note, or prompt into a structured issue
  • Public QR for contractors and visitors, no login
  • Auto-assign an owner. Convert to an action. Critical alerts that break through silent mode
TerraLink. Same capture. US OSHA logs are replaced by WorkSafeBC (and Alberta WCB where the site is Calgary). Categories include spill, near miss, vehicle incident, injury, product damage, and a hazard at a customer’s farm.

03 Asset management

Build
  • Register with types, condition, and history
  • QR tag opens history, schedule, and the pre-start check
  • Maintenance by odometer, engine hours, or time. Work orders
  • Telematics Integrate
  • Bluetooth tool tags Skip
TerraLink. Trucks, tenders, sprayers, nurse tanks, forklifts, blend equipment, extinguishers, eyewash. Samsara is confirmed: vehicles, meters, driver inspection reports, and harsh-driving events come in. Samsara stays the fleet system of record.

04 Sensors and IoT

Integrate
  • Temperature and humidity sensors, threshold alerts, readings tied to a site or asset
TerraLink. Not in the pilot. The database tables are reserved so a cold-chain feed can be added later without a rebuild. Useful later for chemical storage, seed, and biologicals.

05 Lone worker

Build
  • Timed check-ins. A miss escalates up a contact chain
  • SOS and silent alarm. GPS only while a session is active
  • Live monitoring dashboard
  • Bluetooth panic buttons Integrate
Adjustment. Upgraded from build-lite. Full sessions, escalation by push, text, and phone call, and phone-based fall detection. Hardware panic buttons stay out of version one.

06 Contractor management

Build
  • Company register, required documents, inductions by QR
  • Insurance and licence expiry. AI review of a certificate
  • Self-service portal and renewal reminders
TerraLink. Custom applicators, haulers, and trades. WorkSafeBC clearance letter and insurance. Live list of who is on site.
Act · execute

07 Tasks and actions

Build
  • From an inspection, issue, alert, chart, or investigation — or from scratch
  • Owner, due date, priority, site, asset, photos, custom types
  • Recurring actions, overdue chasing, a thread on each action
TerraLink. Types include hazard fix, maintenance, regulatory, and customer. A failed critical inspection item can open the action and take the asset out of service.

08 Communications (Heads Up)

Build
  • Broadcast to a region, site, group, or person
  • Video, image, PDF. Comments. Read receipt and signature on critical notices
TerraLink. Recalls, label changes, heat and wildfire-smoke alerts, policy updates. A morning toolbox talk can be generated from recent near misses and the weather.

09 Documents

Build
  • Versions, owners, review dates, permissions, offline copies
  • QR on site opens the file. Read-and-sign
  • Ask a question and get an answer from the documents you are allowed to see
TerraLink. Safe work procedures, emergency plans, and a safety-data-sheet register with a QR on the shelf. Procedures can also be structured steps, run on the phone like an inspection.

10 AI assistant and agents

Build
  • Chat over the data the person is allowed to see. Drafts checklists, courses, and reports
  • Background agents. A person approves before anything is written
  • Mitti meters this as monthly AI credits Skip
TerraLink. Same assistant and approval queue. Cost is tracked, not rationed per seat. Claude for the writing, a separate speech service for voice, a separate voice for course narration.
Improve · learn

11 Training

Build
  • Course builder, AI course from a document, quizzes, certificates, offline play
  • Training matrix, expiring credentials, toolbox talks, spaced repetition
  • Mitti’s 1,000-course library and 100-language narration Skip. We generate our own courses with AI tools instead: from a prompt, a document, or a walkthrough, and a course can be in more than one language.
TerraLink. 15 courses drafted: orientation, WHMIS, TDG for drivers, pesticides, ammonia, forklift, lockout, spill, heat and smoke, fatigue, working alone, first-response muster, PPE, lifting, incident reporting. Languages: English first, French and Punjabi ready to fill in. Leaderboards can wait.

12 Investigations

Build
  • A case that links the issue, the inspection, and the files
  • Root cause, corrective actions, follow-up
  • Mitti’s OSHA log export Skip
TerraLink. WorkSafeBC employer investigation (48-hour preliminary, 30-day full), claim and return-to-work, and the joint health and safety committee, including the 21-day response timer. Alberta uses the OHS Act serious-incident rules and the 72-hour WCB report.

13 Analytics and branch score

Build
  • Dashboard builder, drill-down, create an action from a chart
  • One 1-to-5 star score per site, with notes on how to improve
TerraLink. Branch league table from inspections, actions, training, credentials, and maintenance. A read-only path for Power BI later. Certificate of Recognition evidence can be pulled from the same records.
Platform

14 Admin and governance

Build
  • Site tree, groups, full / lite / guest seats, permission sets
  • Microsoft sign-in, and automatic user provisioning
  • Activity log. Org-wide defaults
Adjustment. The activity log is kept, not capped at 30–180 days. Staging is the sandbox. Provisioning from Microsoft is in scope once an admin registers the app.

15 Integrations

Build-lite
  • Our own API and webhooks, so other tools can connect
  • Calendar feed for each person
  • Samsara Integrate
  • Named HR connectors and a 1,000-app marketplace Skip
TerraLink. Samsara is the one live connector. Sales Dashboard shares customer and farm-site hazards. Ag Retail Intelligence can share weather and field shapes. No HR system to sync until one is named.

16 Commercial extras

Skip
  • Mitti Insurance
  • Public template and course marketplaces
  • Sensor shop, 24/7 vendor support
  • Per-seat AI credit packs
TerraLink. None of these. We seed our own templates and courses, and we track AI cost without metering it.

How it is built

Stack

  • Web: React, Vite, and Tailwind, hosted as a static site. White surfaces, forest green #3f4b28.
  • Phone: Expo for iPhone and Android. Works offline, then syncs.
  • Background service: PDFs, schedules, email, text, and AI.
  • Database in Canada, with row-level security so people only see their branches.
  • AI: Claude for writing and vision. A separate service for speech-to-text and for reading courses aloud.
  • Alerts: phone push, text message, and email. Critical lone-worker alerts can break through silent mode.

Pilot, in this order

  • Truck pre-trip inspections
  • Vehicle incidents
  • Driver licences: TDG, Class 1 or 3, air brakes
  • Dangerous-goods load check
  • Samsara connection

Left out

  • Mitti Insurance and public marketplaces
  • Per-seat billing and AI credit packs
  • Bluetooth panic buttons, tool tags, and man-down hardware
  • LoRaWAN sensors until there is a reason to buy them
  • A catalogue of HR-system connectors

Still needed from TerraLink

The five questions from 7 October are answered. These are the items that still change what gets turned on.

To start the pilot

  • A Microsoft 365 admin, to turn on single sign-on
  • A read-only Samsara token
  • The branch list, the staff roster, the logo, and the web address

To turn conditional features on or off

  • Does any branch store ammonium nitrate or anhydrous ammonia, and how much?
  • Is there custom spraying?
  • Pesticide vendor licences, and which sites are AWSA-certified
  • Is a Certificate of Recognition being pursued?
  • Which languages seasonal staff need
